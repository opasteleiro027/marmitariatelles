# Catalog

Owns categories, products, images, complement groups and date-specific Sunday
menus. Soft deletion preserves order history.

Marmita sizes are products connected to five builder groups: base, beans,
protein, side and extra. Each size overrides the protein and side selection
limits. Options can be active, inactive or temporarily sold out and may add an
integer-cent amount to the configured marmita.

The admin may edit or soft-delete regular products, builder sizes and builder
options. Deleted entries disappear from the storefront and management list,
while their names and prices remain available in historical order snapshots.
